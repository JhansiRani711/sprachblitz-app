# Privacy Policy

**Sprachblitz** respects your privacy.

## What We Store
- **Email address** — used only for account login and password recovery
- **Learning progress** — XP, streaks, completed lessons, exam scores
- **Cached content** — audio files and vocabulary stored on your device for offline access

## What We Don't Do
- We never sell, share, or trade your data
- We don't use your data for advertising or analytics
- Other users cannot see your progress or learning data
- Your account is completely private to you

## Data Security
- All communication with our servers uses HTTPS encryption
- Your account is protected by Firebase Authentication (Google)
- Your progress is stored in a secure Firebase database

## Offline Data
Audio files and vocabulary cached on your device are yours to keep. They're not encrypted because you own them once downloaded — this is the same as saving a file to your computer.
